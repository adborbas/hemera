Your Home Assistant, live on iPhone and iPad.

Hemera is a dashboard for the smart home you have already built. It connects straight to your own Home Assistant server: no account to create, no cloud in between.

YOUR HOME, YOUR LAYOUT
Pin the devices you use most to a tile grid. Drag to reorder and resize tiles; your layout saves as you go. On first launch, Hemera suggests a layout from your devices to start from.

EVERY ROOM, ALREADY THERE
Your Home Assistant areas and floors show up as they are. Open a room to see its devices by category, and hide the ones you don't need.

ANY COLOR YOU LIKE
Pick a color and every screen follows.

LIVE, NOT REFRESHED
Changes appear the moment they happen on your server. No polling, no pull-to-refresh.

CONTROLS THAT FIT THE DEVICE
Set brightness and color temperature. Move blinds to any position. Adjust target temperature, fan mode and presets, and see each room's temperature and humidity over time. Start, pause or dock a robot vacuum. Toggle switches and run scenes, scripts and automations in one tap. Every device's sensors are one tap away.

READY FOR THE WALL
Kiosk Mode keeps a wall-mounted iPad awake and shows a screensaver when nobody is using it.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
MIT licensed. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays between your device and your Home Assistant server.
